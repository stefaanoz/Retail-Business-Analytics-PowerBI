# 📊 Retail Business Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Retail Business Analytics Dashboard is a demo project developed using Microsoft Power BI to explore retail sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

The dashboard transforms retail data into interactive visual reports to help users monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and explore business performance across different dimensions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Compare sales and profit across categories and customer segments.
- Identify top-selling and most profitable products.
- Analyze customer activity and customer value.
- Explore regional and country-level performance.
- Track year-over-year performance and trends.
- Build an interactive, multi-page Power BI report.

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Analysis
- Total Sales and Total Sales Last Year
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth
- Average Sales per Order
- Sales by Category and Segment
- Top 5 Selling Products by Year
- Monthly Sales Comparison
- Sales by Country

### 2. Profit Analysis
- Profit by Category and Segment
- Top 5 Profitable Products
- Monthly Profit Comparison
- Profitability trends and performance indicators

### 3. Customer & Demographic Analysis
- Total Customers, Orders, and Products
- Average Sales per State
- Top 10 Active Buyers
- Top 10 Valuable Customers
- Top 3 Valuable States with dynamic ranking
- Profit by Order Priority
- Sales and Profit by Region
